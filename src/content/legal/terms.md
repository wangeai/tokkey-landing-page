## 2. Description of the Services

Tokkey is a desktop client whose core capability is optimizing local model inference to reduce memory requirements. The client itself is free and is currently available for Mac. Router is a paid usage-based service, with no charge for requests routed to local models. There are two ways to use Tokkey:

- **Built-in Local Agent.** Inside the Tokkey client, the Local Agent uses local models only. Your prompts, files, and task context are processed on your Mac and are not uploaded to Tokkey or cloud model providers. This mode requires no login and does not offer cloud models or Router.
- **Third-party agents.** In tools such as Codex and Claude Code, you may choose Tokkey Local, Tokkey Cloud, or Tokkey Router. Tokkey Local runs inference on your Mac. Tokkey Cloud uses supported cloud model providers. Router selects between local and cloud models according to the task and your choices. Cloud and Router require a Tokkey account.

Local operation depends on compatible hardware and installed models and tools. Cloud features, downloads, and online tools require connectivity. Model performance and compatibility depend on your device, model, settings, and workload.
