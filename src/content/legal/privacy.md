# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026  

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

This Policy explains how **OneToken Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.** ("One Token Intelligent," "Tokkey," "we," "us," or "our"), incorporated in Hong Kong, handles personal information through the Tokkey Mac application and related online services. We are responsible for the personal information we process as described here. Our separate [Terms of Service](/terms) govern use of the Services.

Tokkey is a free desktop client focused on reducing memory requirements for local model inference. Router is charged by usage, except for requests routed to local models. The built-in Local Agent keeps task content on your Mac. External-agent use of Tokkey Local, Cloud or Router sends task content to Tokkey for 30-day retention; Router content is also used for routing-model training by default. Further details are set out in Section 2.

This Policy covers Tokkey's own handling of personal information. It does not replace the privacy policies of independently operated agents, sign-in services, payment services, or cloud model providers. In particular, information a third-party agent sends independently is different from information Tokkey receives to process a request.

## 1. Personal Information We Process

**Account information.** We use your email address and authentication information to support email or Google sign-in and manage your account.

**Payments and usage.** Stripe processes top-up payments. We process transaction, balance, and billable usage information to administer purchases and charges. The payment information you provide to Stripe is handled under its own [Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). Billing and usage records are separate from the content of your cloud requests.

**Support and technical information.** When you contact us, we process the contact details, messages, and any attachments you choose to provide. Please avoid including passwords, credentials, or unrelated sensitive information in support messages. When you use online features, we may process technical information generated in connection with those features, such as connection details, device and app information, and usage or diagnostic logs. We use this information to provide, maintain, secure and troubleshoot the Services.

**Task content.** Local and external-agent task content is handled differently, as explained in Section 2. Permission to access a file locally does not by itself mean that the file is uploaded to us. Search and upload functions in external agents may send selected material or queries to the services involved. The built-in Local Agent does not upload task content.

Task content may include information about other people as well as yourself. Provide only information you have permission to use, and consider whether personal or confidential information is necessary for the requested task before sending it to an online service.

## 2. Local and Online Data Processing

### Built-in Local Agent

When you use the Local Agent inside the Tokkey client, only local models are available. Your prompts, files, and task context are processed on your Mac and are not uploaded to Tokkey or cloud model providers. You do not need a Tokkey account, and cannot select cloud models or Router in this mode.

Tokkey does not collect or retain this local task content on our servers. The built-in Local Agent may retrieve online information, but does not send your prompts, files, or task context to external services and does not upload files to the cloud. Network connections require technical requests and can disclose connection information, such as an IP address, to the contacted service; this is different from sending task content. Contacting support or downloading a model is also separate from local inference.

### Third-party agents

Tokkey can also be used through compatible third-party AI tools, such as Codex and Claude Code, which connect to Tokkey for model inference. In this mode, you can choose from three options: Tokkey Local, which runs model inference on your Mac; Tokkey Cloud, which sends inference requests to supported third-party cloud model providers for processing; and Tokkey Router, which selects between local and cloud models based on the task and your settings. Tokkey Cloud and Tokkey Router require you to sign in to a Tokkey account, while Tokkey Local can be used without signing in.

These options determine where model inference is performed. The transmission, retention and training use of task content when Tokkey is accessed through third-party tools, including when Tokkey Local is selected, are described below.

Third-party agents may independently collect or transmit information under their own policies and settings. Choosing a local model does not stop those separate activities. The built-in Local Agent's local-only privacy assurance does not apply to this usage mode.

### External-agent content and Router training

When you use Tokkey Local, Tokkey Cloud, or Tokkey Router through an external agent, task content, including prompts, attachments, conversation context, and responses, is sent to Tokkey and retained on our servers for 30 days, then deleted. This includes external-agent use of Tokkey Local even though model inference runs on your Mac. It does not apply to local task content in the built-in Local Agent, which Tokkey does not collect or retain on our servers.

Data received through Tokkey Router is used by default to train and improve Router's model-selection decisions. This trains routing behavior, not models that generate answers. The routing-training use described here applies to Router data; the 30-day retention rule also covers direct Tokkey Local and Tokkey Cloud use through external agents and does not, by itself, mean that content is used for routing training. For requests concerning this training use, see Section 7.

The distinction is between content that stays on your device and content received by Tokkey, not simply whether the model ultimately selected is local or cloud-based. A Router request routed to a local model is free of usage charges, but that billing rule does not exempt Router data received by Tokkey from the training use described here.

## 3. How We Use Personal Information

We use the information available to us to:

- Authenticate users and provide requested online features.
- Train and improve model-selection routing using data received through Tokkey Router, with participation enabled by default.
- Manage balances, payments, usage charges, and billing questions.
- Respond to support requests and resolve service issues.
- Protect accounts and online Services from fraud or abuse.
- Send necessary account, transaction, security, and service notices.
- Meet applicable legal obligations and address legal claims.

We do not sell personal information or use or share it for advertising, including targeted advertising. These purposes do not authorize us to retrieve task content that remains solely on your Mac.

The main relationships between information and purpose are:

|Information|Purpose|
|---|---|
|Email address and authentication information|Sign-in and account administration|
|Transactions, balance, and billable usage|Top-ups, charging, and billing support|
|Content received for online requests|Processing the requested operation|
|Data received through Router|Training model-selection routing by default, in addition to processing Router requests; not training models to generate answers|
|Support messages and submitted attachments|Responding to the issue you report|
|Technical information, including connection details, device and app information, and usage or diagnostic logs|Providing, maintaining, securing and troubleshooting the Services|

Describing one purpose does not authorize an unrelated use. In particular, the use of Router data for model-selection training is disclosed separately from providing an inference response.

## 4. Disclosure and Third-Party Services

Relevant providers may process information needed for authentication, payments, cloud inference, and other requested service functions. Google supports the sign-in option you choose; Stripe processes payments. Cloud providers receive the content needed for cloud requests. A third-party agent's independent processing is governed by its own privacy policy. For providers processing personal information on our behalf, we require confidentiality, security and data-retention safeguards appropriate to the relevant services and applicable law. The location of processing depends on the online service and provider used and may be outside your country or region. Where required by applicable law, we apply safeguards for international transfers.

A provider's role determines the information it needs for that function. Using Google sign-in or Stripe payments does not, by itself, authorize sending those providers your AI conversation content. Likewise, a file permission granted on your Mac is not a general authorization for every external service to receive the file.

We may disclose information we hold where required by law or valid legal process, or where legally permitted and necessary to protect the Services or establish or defend legal claims. This does not give us access to local files we do not possess.

Cloud model providers and third-party agents have their own collection, retention, and training practices. Tokkey's 30-day content retention period does not determine how long those independent services retain information.

Third-party services may process information independently of Tokkey under their own terms, privacy policies and settings. To the extent they act independently and not on our behalf, we do not control their collection, use, retention or disclosure of information, and those activities are not governed by this Policy. This includes independent processing by third-party AI tools when you use Tokkey Local; the built-in Local Agent’s local-only privacy assurance does not extend to those tools. You should review the relevant third-party policies and settings before using their services or sharing personal or confidential information. Our own disclosures of personal information to those services remain subject to this Policy.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

User content received by Tokkey when you use Tokkey Local, Tokkey Cloud, or Tokkey Router through an external agent is retained for 30 days and then deleted. This describes retention of the content itself; it does not mean a trained routing model is deleted after 30 days. Any personal information we continue to hold or control remains subject to applicable retention requirements and privacy rights. Records that must be retained under applicable law, such as required accounting records, remain subject to those legal requirements. We retain account, billing and support information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including applicable legal, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements.

Signing out, deleting an account, uninstalling the client, and deleting local files are different actions. Account closure does not necessarily remove downloaded models, conversation history, or other files on your Mac. Use local controls to remove local data. Deleting local files does not itself close an online account or erase records held independently by third parties.

The 30-day period concerns external-agent task content held by Tokkey, whether you select Local, Cloud, or Router. It is not a 30-day expiry date for a local model or a file on your device, and does not determine the retention period for legally required accounting records. To request deletion of personal information we hold or closure of your account, use the email contact in Section 7.

Where applicable law requires separate consent for routing-model training, acceptance of our Terms or this Policy alone does not provide that consent. The default participation described above does not override those requirements or applicable privacy rights.
